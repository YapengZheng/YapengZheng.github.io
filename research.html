Research Interests

  • Network Formation and Peer Effects: Studying how social and economic networks form and how peer effects influence individual behavior and outcomes.
  • Synthetic Control Methods: Developing and applying synthetic control methodologies for causal inference in policy evaluation.
  • High-Dimensional Statistics: Working with statistical methods for analyzing data where the number of variables is large relative to the sample size.
  • Panel Data Analysis: Analyzing longitudinal data to understand dynamic relationships and causal effects.
